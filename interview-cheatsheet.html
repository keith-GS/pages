The Crater Lake Interviews

Lloyd & Helen — 60 Years · quick, lively, and full of laughs
Laughs first · then the finale lands

1 The 30-second setup

  • Shoot horizontal. Turn the phone sideways. Person a little off-center, the lake or lodge behind them.
  • Brace it on a railing, rock, car roof - anything steady. Don't walk-and-talk.
  • Golden hour (first hour after sunrise, last before sunset) makes everything look cinematic.
  • Count to 3 in silence before and after each answer - gives me clean edit handles.
  • Ask them to start the answer with the question in it ("The secret is…") so it stands on its own.
  • Keep it short. One story, one or two sentences. Short is gold.

2 Get them laughing (lead with these)

These come right before the tender ending - so warm the room up. Go for the grin.

What drives you nuts about each other after 60 years?
Who's the better driver?  (ask them both, at the same time)
What's the most trouble you two ever got into?
Tell me about the Chevy.  /  Tell me about the bike.
What's the worst gift he/she ever gave you?
To the kids: What did you get away with that they never found out about?  (until now)

3 Lloyd & Helen — the heart

How did you two meet?
When did you know the other one was the one?
What's the secret to sixty years?
What are you proudest of?
Finish this: "After sixty years, love is…"

4 Kids, foster kids, grandkids & friends

Finish this: "Lloyd and Helen are…"
What did they teach you?
Tell one story that says everything about them.
What did it mean to have a place at their table?

5 The finale question — ask EVERYONE

"What do you want to say to them on their 60th?"

Same question, everyone, look right into the lens. This is the ending of the film - the whole room appears one after another. Get the little kids too. Get Larry (just keep Lloyd in frame with him).
