a quick note, just from me -

If you can stretch the budget, get the Mac. Here's why.

I'm not a Mac fanatic. I use a PC for work most days. But for what you actually do at home - emails, photos, YouTube rabbit holes, music, video calls with the family, the occasional tax document - the MacBook Air is just plainly the nicer thing to live with.

Three reasons specifically for you:

Music sounds better on it. You're the person in our family most likely to be listening to something good while doing something else. The six speakers in the Air actually fill a room. Even with no headphones, your kitchen will sound nicer.

It plays well with your iPhone. Photos show up automatically. You can answer texts from the laptop. AirPods just work. If you have an iPhone (you do), the rest of the Apple stuff fits together like Lego.

It will outlast the PCs by years. Macs age slower. The $400 difference between the HP and the Mac stretches across 6-8 years, which is roughly $50 a year for "I love using this thing" - which is a very good deal.

If the money matters more than the experience, get the HP - it's a real laptop and you'll be fine. But if you're on the fence, I'd push you toward the Mac every time.
