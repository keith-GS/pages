Feature Spotlight · New this week.

Every number on Reports, traced to its source

Open any of the six live Reports and click a figure. A panel opens beside the report and shows exactly where that number came from.

  • How it is worked out. Each calculated figure says in plain words what goes into it.
  • Every input. The round, the investor and the amount behind the total: five checks from three financing documents in the example below.
  • The document itself. Each input opens the document it was read from, so a question in the partner meeting gets answered in the meeting.
  • Headline numbers too. The totals at the top of Portfolio Positions open their working the same way.

More reports are on the way, starting with the Liquidation Preference Stack below. If there is a report you want us to include, reply and let us know.
