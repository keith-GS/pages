Feature Spotlight · New this week.

Reports: report on every position in the portfolio

Reporting is live. Reports is a new surface where every report opens presentation-grade - letterhead and disclosures included - and flips between a print-styled document and a working sheet you can export. Lead vs Follow and Unpriced Exposure & Maturity Ladder are live today, Portfolio Positions is in final review, and more are on the way. All of it is built from your documents automatically. If there is a report you want us to include, reply to this email and let us know.

Keith's Note

Everything in this email runs on the same raw material: your documents. We started at the back-office end of that, because getting from a pile of charters and financing agreements to audit-ready valuations is the hard, unglamorous part. But the investor graph that falls out of those same documents - who you invest alongside, who leads and who follows, who shows up round after round - is exactly the data a GP needs to source the next deal and raise the next fund. We are already exploring both with a handful of you, and those conversations are shaping what we build next. The crux is simple: it is your data, and we want to bring it to life for you in every way we can. We have not found all of those ways yet; if you have one in mind, reply and tell us.

What Shipped This Week

52 ships in fourteen days.

 
 Reports launched: a new surface where every report opens presentation-grade (letterhead and disclosures included), flips to a working sheet, and exports - built from your documents automatically. Two reports live today, more on the way - and report metrics now wait for data instead of flashing a definitive zero while it loads.
 
 Lead vs Follow, a new report: rounds led versus followed - average check, share of round, ownership held, and capital deployed, side by side. The fundraising story every GP assembles by hand.
 
 Unpriced Exposure & Maturity Ladder, a new report: every SAFE, convertible note, venture debt facility and warrant that has not yet converted, been repaid or been exercised - principal, cap, discount, rate, strike and maturity, laddered by quarter.
 
 Investor Graph: firm-family grouping. Co-investors are grouped under their firm family, so one firm's fund entities read as one firm on the leaderboard, signals and network view rather than six separate rows.
 
 Investor Graph: polish pass. The view is named consistently, the firm panel no longer opens a tooltip over its own header, panel links land on filtered pages, and long company names are no longer truncated.
 
 Investor Graph: classification fix. Institutional funds are no longer labeled “Angel” in Co-investor Intelligence.
 
 Governance: Board of Directors. Each company's Governance tab now shows who holds every board seat, which party has the contractual right to appoint it, the source document behind each seat, and a timeline toggle to see the composition as it stood on any date.
 
 Governance: Board Structure and Seat composition. The authorized board rendered as a real table from the Charter - seat type, count, and designating party - instead of a flat seat count.
 
 Affiliate secondary transfers move the position. When two of your own funds transfer shares between them, GoodStream moves the position instead of booking a fresh purchase - so shares and paid-in capital are never counted twice. A request straight from your feedback.
 
 Current Holdings reduces the seller on a secondary. When one of your funds is the seller on a secondary transfer, its position is reduced accordingly.
 
 Capitalization: within-round warrant, conversion and stock events. Warrant exercises, conversions and stock events that land inside a financing round are now folded into that round's snapshot instead of being skipped.
 
 Restating charters handled: a charter that only restates existing terms no longer registers as a new priced round that reprices every held class.
 
 Convertible conversions reconciled: a conversion that spans multiple instruments now lands as one position in Current Holdings, not two.
 
 Securities & Waterfall: no dropped classes. Series Seed and Series A classes are no longer dropped from a Charter snapshot when the Charter restates them.
 
 Current Holdings: one class per group. Two different preferred share classes are no longer totaled under one class's header, which had overstated that class's shares and cost basis.
 
 Our Position caption matches the mark. On shadow series, the Capitalization Our Position card now shows the held class's own price alongside the round price, so the two never contradict each other.
 
 Capitalization: fewer false flags. The unaccounted-shares flag no longer fires when a cap table carries no holder-position data.
 
 Secondary sale reconciliation. Secondary sales can no longer exceed the recorded holding for a class - the data quality gap that caused it is closed.
 
 Performance: net cash burn and Runway. Net burn is now operating plus investing cash flow with the sign normalized (financing is excluded), so Runway renders when a company is actually burning instead of going blank.
 
 Accounting parentheses read right: a value printed as (1,234) now lands as a negative on Performance instead of being dropped.
 
 Scale captions respected: a statement noting “figures in thousands” now scales every value it covers instead of rendering them 1,000x too small.
 
 Trailing-twelve-month totals fixed: the LTM view no longer sums overlapping periods into an inflated total.
 
 Margin labels resolved: bare “Total COS” and “Total Gross Margin” lines now map to the right metrics, so COGS, gross profit, and gross margin populate.
 
 Performance: bare “Total” lines resolved. Income-statement section totals captured as a bare “Total” now populate the metric they belong to.
 
 Partial periods guarded: a June year-to-date is never offered as a full-year value in Performance cells.
 
 Performance: year-to-date rows roll up correctly. YTD cumulative rows no longer enter quarterly, annual and trailing-twelve-month roll-ups as if they were discrete quarterly flows.
 
 Performance: Headcount (FTE) populated. Headcount is no longer blank when the monthly financials report it.
 
 Older periods in reach: the Performance KPI table now scrolls horizontally through your full history.
 
 Faster, steadier Performance loads: no more empty-skeleton hangs, and the Annual view loads reliably.
 
 One place for provenance: the on-cell hover picker is gone - the drawer is now the single place to inspect and choose the source behind a value.
 
 Performance: “Use this value” persists. Choosing a value in the provenance drawer now saves reliably instead of reporting success and reverting.
 
 Performance: cleaner legend. The “future period held” legend was removed from the Performance tab.
 
 Salesforce: import a round the moment legal marks it complete. A round is picked up, processed and handed back for review as soon as it is marked complete in Salesforce - late-arriving closing binders no longer need a manual re-scan.
 
 Re-extract a single field: re-run specific fields on an already-processed document without a full reprocess, with every replacement traceable.
 
 GoodBot keeps up with the product: ask it about anything we shipped recently, including everything in this email.
 
 Fund Matching, deduplicated: case-variant duplicates of the same fund now reconcile to a single entity feeding Fund Matching.
 
 Fund Matching: redesigned matching-rules panel. The rules that decide which positions belong to which fund are laid out more clearly.
 
 Portfolio filters stay in their workspace. Filters set in one workspace no longer persist into every other workspace.
 
 Agent Log filters fixed: “Filter by kind” now works and resets properly with Clear filters.
 
 Sturdier cloud imports: a transient provider error now retries instead of losing the file terminally.
 
 Large Excel financial packets no longer time out. Multi-period spreadsheets that previously stalled during extraction now complete, and reprocessing recovers them.
 
 Review Queue catches disagreements on required fields. A document can no longer be marked Complete with nothing extracted; required-field disagreements between our two models are always routed to human review.
 
 Review Queue catches empty protected fields. A run can no longer complete with a required protected field (such as Effective Date) left empty without raising a Review Queue flag.
 
 Model bench: evaluated a new candidate model against our extraction benchmarks before letting it near your documents.
 
 Extraction reliability: fixed a model-selection issue on OCR-fed documents and an accuracy-scoring gap in our internal evals.
 
 Daily automated QA: a daily sweep now checks rendering, data quality, and persistence across the app.
 
 QA catch, Aug 26: two regressions caught by the daily sweep, fixed and closed.
 
 QA catch, Aug 28: seven more caught and cleared the same way.
 
 Signup and billing notifications. Our team is now notified on every new signup and every billing event, so onboarding starts sooner.
 
 SOC 2 cadence: completed the monthly third-party app access review - part of the SOC 2 Type II audit in progress.
 
 SOC 2 tooling: the monthly review is now a repeatable, evidenced control with its own runbook.
 
 Dependency hygiene: patched 13 transitive vulnerabilities in our tooling.
What's Next · Rolling Out

In final review now, rolling out soon.

 
 In-app notifications and email digests. A notifications tab in the app - every change GoodStream makes to your portfolio, as it happens, with each company one click away - plus a daily email digest so you are never sent one email per document.
 
 Reconciliation status on every portfolio company. A clear signal on each company telling you where its numbers stand: Verified, In review, or waiting on something from the company (Documents needed or Answers needed) - with the as-of date the status was last confirmed.
 
 Realized Proceeds and Remaining Basis on Current Holdings. Proceeds from secondary transfers, convertible transfers and redemptions surfaced on every position - one of your requests.
 
 Portfolio Positions report. Every position in the portfolio, grouped and sliced by fund, stage or lead investor - the next addition to Reports, with more reports behind it.
 
 Board contacts on Contacts. Directors and their appointing parties, surfaced on the Contacts surfaces alongside your co-investors.
 
 Convertible discount gain, shown. Shadow series marked as-converted at the primary round price, so the discount you earned on a converted note is visible on the mark.
 
 Fund Matching honored everywhere. A fund switched off in Fund Matching is no longer treated as your deal in portfolio attribution.
 
 Google Drive reconnect, fixed. One reconnect repairs every source on a connection, and a Shared-Drive-only permission error is no longer mislabeled as a revoked grant.
 
 Performance accuracy, round three. Same-document precedence decided by a stated ladder, section totals for COGS and S&M instead of a component line, and a guard against pinning a partial period as a full year.
 
 Salesforce round-trip to production. Reviewed data pushed back to Salesforce, with in-progress review work preserved and two people kept from colliding on it.
